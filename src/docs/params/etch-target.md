## Physical meaning

The target identifies the material the geometric etch rule removes at its nominal amount. Other occupied materials are removed only according to the selectivity rule; the name does not select a plasma chemistry or calculate reaction products.

## Failure modes

- A wrong target can leave the intended film uncleared while attacking a mask, spacer, or stop layer.
- A target that is absent along the chosen cut can make the step appear inactive even when it intersects a different layout location.

## Compare with

Switch between the intended film and its mask or stop material without changing depth to verify that material assignment matches the process story.
