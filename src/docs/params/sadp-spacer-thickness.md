## Physical meaning

Spacer thickness sets the lateral width of the sidewall-derived lines in the simplified SADP construction. A conformal real film and anisotropic etch both influence final spacer CD, but this input represents their net geometry rather than simulating either unit process.

## Failure modes

- A thin spacer provides a narrow transfer mask and can exaggerate grid quantization in the simulator.
- A thick spacer can consume the available gap and merge neighboring regions.
